# Stage Director: proposal (hybrid)

Produce `proposal_packet`, locking `delivery_promise: hybrid_motion_still`
(the pipeline default) with a genuine editorial rationale.

## What makes a good hybrid proposal

- State explicitly *why* a still/motion mix serves this specific story
  better than continuous motion — e.g. "the piece unfolds as a series of
  remembered moments; held stills give each memory weight before the next
  motion beat moves time forward."
- Estimate roughly what fraction of shots will be stills vs. motion, and
  make sure that estimate clears the `hybrid_motion_still` minimum motion
  fraction (40%) with margin — planning for exactly the minimum leaves no
  room for a shot or two failing to generate as motion later.
- If, after considering it, the concept actually wants to be nearly all
  motion, say so and recommend `cinematic` instead rather than forcing a
  `hybrid` promise that doesn't fit; if it wants to be almost all stills,
  recommend the human consider a `slideshow` promise explicitly rather than
  mislabeling it `hybrid`.

## Human approval

Because the delivery promise here is a genuine creative bet (not just the
pipeline's obvious default), be especially thorough in `concept_summary` —
the human approving this stage needs to understand *why* stillness is being
used, not just that it will be.
