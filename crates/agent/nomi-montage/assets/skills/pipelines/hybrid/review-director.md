# Stage Director: review (hybrid)

Produce an honest `final_review` against the seven-dimension rubric. For
hybrid work the decisive questions are:

- Does the still/motion rhythm feel **chosen**, or does it feel like a
  motion pipeline that ran out of budget?
- Do held stills earn their screen time (composition strong enough to hold
  alone), or do they read as filler between motion clips?
- Does the piece still satisfy the locked `delivery_promise` once you
  watch the composed cut end to end?

Score each dimension 0–10 from what you actually observe in the render,
not from what the proposal hoped for. Set `pass` false when the weighted
total falls below the publish quality gate, even if every stage earlier
"succeeded."
