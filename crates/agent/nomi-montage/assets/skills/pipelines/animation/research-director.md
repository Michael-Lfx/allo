# Stage Director: research (animation)

Produce `research_brief` that grounds later creative work.

## Priorities
Gather visual and tonal references for character/world consistency. This is reference research, not market research — capture palette, silhouette, and mood anchors.

## Rules
- At least three concrete findings with sources or explicit low-confidence
  labels.
- Separate facts from creative hypotheses.
- Stay relevant to the project prompt and intended audience.
