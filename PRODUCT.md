# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Flowy serves people who want one desktop workspace for conversations, files,
shells, browsers, scheduled work, local agents, and remote capabilities. They
may be technical or non-technical, but they expect to describe an outcome,
understand what the agent is doing, and recover when attention is required.

## Product Purpose

Flowy turns an intended result into visible, controllable work. The interface
should help users start quickly, follow progress without supervising every
step, inspect evidence when needed, and retain confidence that local data and
desktop actions remain under their control.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, precise, alive.

Flowy should feel like a capable working surface rather than a chat toy. At
rest it is calm and legible. During interaction it responds immediately and
makes direction, progress, and state changes easy to follow.

## Anti-references

- A cold document canvas where every state change is an instantaneous color swap.
- A wall of equal cards with no clear object or action hierarchy.
- Neon, glass, glow, gradients, or looping motion used as decoration.
- Consumer-chat styling that hides tools, files, permissions, and recovery paths.
- Theme-specific interaction behavior that changes the meaning of controls.

## Design Principles

- Put the current task and its next action ahead of product chrome.
- Preserve spatial and causal continuity between an action and its result.
- Use familiar desktop affordances, then add personality through precision and rhythm.
- Let themes change atmosphere while semantic states and behavior remain stable.
- Improve one complete workflow at a time and require real-use acceptance before expansion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Maintain readable text and control states across every built-in light and dark preset.
- Never rely on color alone for running, success, warning, error, or selection state.
- Support keyboard focus and reduced motion throughout core workflows.
- Keep motion short, interruptible, and non-blocking.
