# Stage Director: script (cinematic)

Produce `script` — the scene-by-scene narrative spine that `scene_plan` will
later break into shots. See `skills/creative/storytelling.md` for the
general craft; this note covers what's specific to a cinematic film.

## Cinematic-specific guidance

- Write scenes as things a camera can actually show, not abstract statements.
  "The company values quality" is not scriptable; "a technician holds a
  finished part up to the light, checking a seam by eye" is.
- Favor visual storytelling over expository narration where possible — a
  cinematic piece should be able to carry meaning through image and action,
  with narration/dialogue reinforcing rather than replacing it.
- Keep the emotional arc from `proposal_packet.concept_summary` visible scene
  to scene: an opening that establishes a want/tension, a middle that
  develops it, an ending that resolves or reframes it.

## Checklist before `stage_complete`

- Every scene advances `proposal_packet.key_message` — cut any scene that
  doesn't.
- Total estimated read/watch time roughly matches
  `proposal_packet.estimated_duration_secs`.
- No invented specific claims beyond what `research_brief` supports.
