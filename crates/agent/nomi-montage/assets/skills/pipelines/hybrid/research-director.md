# Stage Director: research (hybrid)

Produce `research_brief`. Follow the same grounding discipline as any other
pipeline (see `skills/pipelines/cinematic/research-director.md` for the
general approach) — nothing about the hybrid delivery format changes what
counts as good research.

## One hybrid-specific consideration

If the topic itself has a natural "archival" or "reflective" quality (a
personal history, a retrospective, a slow transformation over time), note
that explicitly in a finding — it strengthens the case for choosing
`hybrid_motion_still` deliberately at `proposal` rather than defaulting to
it without justification.
