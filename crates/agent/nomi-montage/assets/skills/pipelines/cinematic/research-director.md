# Stage Director: research (cinematic)

Produce `research_brief` — the factual/contextual grounding the creative
proposal will be built on. For a cinematic brand/story film, research means
understanding the subject well enough to write specific, true details into
the script, not generic filler.

## What to actually research

- The concrete subject of the film (a product, a person, an event, a place)
  — what makes it specific and worth a viewer's attention.
- The target audience's likely context: what they already know, what would
  surprise or move them.
- Any constraints from the project prompt (brand guidelines, factual claims
  that must be accurate, things to avoid) — record these under
  `constraints`-equivalent findings so `proposal` doesn't miss them.

## Output discipline

- Every entry in `findings` should be a genuinely useful, specific claim —
  not a restatement of the topic string. "This is a coffee brand" is not a
  finding; "specialty coffee buyers respond more to origin-story
  authenticity than to price messaging" is a finding (if actually
  well-supported — label confidence honestly per
  `assets/schemas/artifacts/research_brief.json`).
- Use `low`/`medium`/`high` confidence labels honestly. A `low`-confidence
  finding is still useful context as long as it's labeled — an unlabeled
  guess presented as fact is not.
- `recommended_angle` should be a genuinely actionable creative direction the
  `proposal` stage can build on, not a restatement of the brief.
